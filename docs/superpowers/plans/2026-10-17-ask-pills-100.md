# Ask Pills 100 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

Status: in progress (2026-10-17).

**Goal:** Grow the Ask the HQ pill bank from 44 to about 100 questions in seven topics (adding "About me" and "This site"), and show a seeded random sample per page session.

**Architecture:** The bank stays data (`content/ask/suggestions.json`). `src/ask/suggestions.ts` gains a seeded sampler (mulberry32 shuffle) for topic expansions and a mixed starter row; follow-ups stay deterministic. The seed lives in `sessionStorage["hq:ask-seed"]` so tests can inject it. Public stats keep their five topics: the two new pill topics map onto existing stats topics, so the snapshot contract does not change.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 static export, React 19, TypeScript, Vitest, Playwright, Bun.

## Global Constraints

- No em dashes (U+2014) anywhere.
- Every pill has EN and natural ID text; EN at most about 48 characters (compact pills, #162); ID as short as natural Indonesian allows.
- No client company names (C3); no pills on declined topics (partisan politics, religion, pay, family, health).
- Every pill's `expect` lists real chunk ids that retrieval returns in both languages.
- Ask knowledge pack stays under `PACK_MAX_BYTES` (256 KiB).
- Follow-ups after an answer stay deterministic (`followUps`, then tag matches, then other topics).
- Each topic has at least 10 pills; total about 100.
