# CI

- Date: 2026-10-23

CI speed without losing coverage or changing a budget. Owner approved: the `main` ruleset drops `3D perf gates 1/2` and `3D perf gates 2/2` from its required checks (it keeps `Typecheck, lint, unit tests`, `Build, CV and e2e` and `Lighthouse CI`), because the aggregate `Build, CV and e2e` already fails unless every e2e and perf shard passed, and the fixed names forced every pull request onto 2 perf shards; `scripts/merge-gate.ts` falls back to the same three names. The perf lane keeps 2 Playwright workers per shard (1 worker per shard and a solo phase for the heavy tests were analysed and left for later). Changes: a full pull request runs 6 e2e and 4 perf shards (at most 10 e2e plus perf shards per pull request run, so its peak stays 12 jobs with `Lighthouse CI` and the unit test job; `main`, dispatches and the nightly keep 8 and 4, peak 14), and a selective pull request sizes its perf shards 1 to 4 from `e2e/durations.json` (`ceil(seconds / 960)`, as a complement run); a perf test missing from `e2e/durations.json` counts as the lane's longest known test instead of the median, each shard lists the missing tests, and the file was refreshed from runs after #291 (46 perf tests instead of 37; the perf lane estimate went from 4057 to 7129 worker-seconds); the setup action bounds apt (15 s timeouts, 3 retries) and installs Playwright's system packages from cached `.deb` files; the bun cache loses its restore keys; the Next build cache is saved only on `main`; the Lighthouse warm-up URL runs once instead of 3 times; the aggregate job merges the blob reports without the full setup. Evidence (46 green CI runs, 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-22): a full pull request took 37.3 min median (p90 43.5), set by `3D perf gates 1/2` at 1982 s while the e2e shards finished about 20 min earlier; the planner estimated 1205 s per perf shard and the shards ran 2010 to 2124 s, because 9 perf tests (the Agent Office long poles among them, planned at 84 s, run at 4 to 16 min) were missing from the durations; 21 of 334 Playwright setups took over 120 s on a stalled Ubuntu mirror (26% of runs, up to 21 min); the bun cache archive was 729 MB for a 0.6 s install; per-PR Next cache saves held 7.7 GB and pushed the repository past the 10 GB Actions cache quota; the warm-up URL took about 70 s of the 481 s Lighthouse step. Expected: a full pull request about 37 to about 20 min (the planned perf shards are about 900 s each, the e2e shards about 750 s), apt stalls no longer on the wall time, about 40 s off Lighthouse and 10 to 15 s off the aggregate job.
