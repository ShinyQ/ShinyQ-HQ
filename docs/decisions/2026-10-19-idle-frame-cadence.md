# 3D experience

- Date: 2026-10-19

Owner item 11: "Idle frame rate: drop to 30 fps or on-demand rendering when the rover is parked, the camera still and nothing animates (always under reduced motion). Animations that run (ambient packets, sky, stars, globe) must still look smooth. If idle scenes always have motion, render the idle cadence at 30 fps only where it isn't visible. Must not break rides, e2e timing or the perf gates." Replaces the "no idle cap" rule. After 1 s without input, store changes or scene motion the canvas renders at the floor's idle cadence. Full motion: 30 fps on L2 and B1, whose parked motion (slow pulses, shader flows, twinkles, the rover's bob) steps under 0.6 CSS px per 60 fps frame; 60 on L1, L3, L4 and RF, whose parked views show the data packets (about 1.4 px per frame, so about 3 px steps at 30 fps), the L3 reactor rings and hologram board, the L4 newsroom ticker and the RF drone (about 1.5 px). Reduced motion: on demand (the rover's blinks and timers, the running day and night clock every 250 ms, a 1 s heartbeat), and 30 fps on L1 for the hologram's slow spin. A per-view check (30 fps on the quiet parts of L1, L3, L4 and RF) was tried and dropped: with it the change added 2.0 KB gzip to the 3D session and went over both caps (1.6 KB of headroom); the per-floor version adds 1.1 KB. Any input or state change returns to 60 fps on the next animation frame; off under automation, so e2e, the perf lane and the virtual clock are unchanged. Measured parked on an M-series GPU (full tier, 1440x900 at 2x): reduced motion 60 to about 2.2 fps on L2 to RF (main thread busy 7 to 22% to about 1.3 to 2.3%), L1 30 fps; full motion L2 60 to 30 fps (busy 16% to 7%).
