# 3D experience

- Date: 2026-10-19
- PR: [#259](https://github.com/ShinyQ/ShinyQ-HQ/pull/259)

Owner item 11: "Idle frame rate: drop to 30 fps or on-demand rendering when the rover is parked, the camera still and nothing animates (always under reduced motion). Animations that run (ambient packets, sky, stars, globe) must still look smooth. If idle scenes always have motion, render the idle cadence at 30 fps only where it isn't visible. Must not break rides, e2e timing or the perf gates." Replaces the "no idle cap" rule. After 1 s without input, store changes or scene motion the canvas renders at the floor's idle cadence. Full motion: 30 fps on L2 and B1, whose parked motion (slow pulses, shader flows, twinkles, the rover's bob) steps under 0.6 CSS px per 60 fps frame; 60 on L1, L3, L4 and RF, whose parked views show the data packets (about 1.4 px per frame, so about 3 px steps at 30 fps), the L3 reactor rings and hologram board, the L4 newsroom ticker and the RF drone (about 1.5 px). Reduced motion: on demand (the rover's antenna and eye blinks, R3F invalidations, the running day and night clock every 250 ms), and 30 fps on L1 for the hologram's slow spin. A per-view check (30 fps on the quiet parts of L1, L3, L4 and RF) was tried and dropped: with it the change added 2.0 KB gzip to the 3D session and went over both caps. Any input or state change returns to 60 fps on the next animation frame; off under automation, so e2e, the perf lane and the virtual clock are unchanged. Measured parked on an M-series GPU (full tier, 1440x900 at 2x): reduced motion 60 to about 2.2 fps on L2 to RF (main thread busy 7 to 22% to about 1.3 to 2.3%), L1 30 fps; full motion L2 60 to 30 fps (busy 16% to 7%).

Owner decision on the byte cost (option A, verbatim): "The owner picked option A for item 11, on one condition: slim the idle detector first. An input-idle timer (passive pointer, key and wheel listeners, one timestamp, a check inside the existing frame loop) should be a few hundred bytes, not 1.1 KB. Find out what makes it big (an imported store, a hook wrapper, duplicated per-floor config) and cut it. Then decide lazy or inline, whichever is honest. Raise both session caps only by what's left after slimming, using the rule: measured value plus about 0.6 KB. Record the before and after bytes in the decision entry." Before: 1.1 KB gzip on the main 3D path (1,136 B; base 639,412 B, with the detector 640,548 B; lite, `?prefetch=off`). After slimming: 0.69 KB gzip (691 B; 640,103 B) on both tiers, inline in the main 3D chunk so the bytes gate counts it (a lazy chunk would load in every visitor session but never in the automated gate). The slim detector reuses the rover's input counter, the store subscription and one camera-matrix check inside the existing frame loop; the per-floor rule is one expression, and the on-demand wakes are the rover's antenna and eye blinks and the running sky. Owner decision at merge (verbatim): "Go with A (inline). It matches the owner's earlier decision ("A, but slim it first; caps rise only by what's left"): less download for everyone, and the first-frame cap moves under the same measured + 0.6 KB rule. Set failAt and both session caps from the post-rebase measurements, record them in the decision entry, and merge PR E when green."
