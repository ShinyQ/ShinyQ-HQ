# Public visit stats: visits before the launch

- Date: 2026-10-23

Owner request: count the visits the site had before the Visitor Observatory existed. The previous portfolio on the same host (`kurniadi.pages.dev`) had its own Cloudflare Web Analytics site (`21d90569...`), and Cloudflare still kept its rows from 2026-04-25 (older data is past the 184-day window). Its days from 2026-04-25 to 2026-10-09 were prepended to the `stats:days:v1` ledger by hand: 40 visits and 340 page views, countries ID, FR, US and DE, queried day by day with the Worker's filters (bot 0, production hosts) because a long window is sampled more coarsely and drops countries. They are sampled estimates (Web Analytics sampled that site at 1 in 10 to 1 in 100), as the public source line already says. The stored days from 2026-10-10 on were not touched (a full rebuild was rejected after a dry run showed a fresh long-window answer does not reproduce them). "Since launch" now reads since 2026-04-25 (all-time 200 visits and 1,111 page views through 2026-10-22); "Last 30 days" is unchanged. The original ledger and snapshot are backed up outside the repo; the method is in `workers/stats/README.md` (Repair).
