# Classic site: quiet availability line

- Date: 2026-11-06

Owner rule (2026-11-06): searching on the Classic site and opening a result must never scroll straight down to "I am open to freelance and part-time software and AI engineering work." ("it seems like I am begging for it"), and this goes into the constitution. Navigation, search and palette results, missions, deep links, CTAs and auto-scroll never land on or scroll to the availability line; it stays a quiet, visitor-found element ([quiet availability line](../agents/content-style-and-safety.md#quiet-availability-line)). Cause: `html` scrolls smoothly (`globals.css`) without `data-scroll-behavior="smooth"`, so Next 16 kept smooth scrolling on during route changes; its scroll-to-top calls `scrollIntoView` on the new page's children from the last to the first, and the smooth scroll toward the last one (the closing `#contact`, whose heading is the availability line) could win, intermittently, on case study and About pages (a local build of main: 4 of 24 palette searches ended at y 2,489 to 7,793 with the line in view). `<html>` now carries the attribute. The Highlights Hall Contact station's Classic path moves from `/#contact` to `/contact`, and the boot card's Contact link from `/contact?view=classic#availability` to `/contact?view=classic`; this replaces the `#availability` target of the 2026-11-01 first screen contact decision.
