# L1 Agent Office

- Date: 2026-10-22

Owner-approved relay throttle, relayed by the coordinator. Cause: the relay's dedupe worked, but a busy agent's desk really changes on most hook events: a shell command's `preToolUse` maps to working (or reviewing, testing) and its `postToolUse` to thinking, so every command sent two beats. The owner's relay log of 2026-10-21 (18:45 to 20:27 UTC, 1.7 h) holds 4,247 hook events and 2,040 beats (48%); of the 2,290 desk events (not permission checks or helper events) 89% were sent, led by `postToolUse thinking` (890), `preToolUse working` (385) and `preToolUse reviewing` (379). The gaps between a session's events are mostly 2 to 14 s, so the first proposal, a 2 s coalescing window, cuts almost nothing: on the replayed busy session (`tests/relay/busy-session.ts`, 304 hook events, 157 desk events, 9.6 min) the relay before sends 97 beats, a 2 s window 91, 5 s 68, 10 s 50 and 15 s 40. Rule, per session: a change (state, activity, helpers, repository or title) goes out at once when the last beat is at least 10 s old (`COALESCE_MS`, equal to the visitors' active poll `POLL.activeMs`, so no change a visitor could see is lost), else a trailing send at the end of the window carries the newest state (a flap back to the sent state sends nothing); final states (stop, session end, failure, Needs Kurniadi) go out at once and cancel a waiting change; an awake session with events since its last beat gets a keepalive 15 s after it (`KEEPALIVE_MS`). The window must stay at or under 15 s: beats of an active desk then stay under the Worker's 20 s thinking rule (`TIMING.thinkingAfter`, unchanged; tested: no rule-made thinking within 20 s of a desk event). The hook still exits at once; the trailing send and keepalive come from one detached `flush` child per session, owned through a token in the existing per-session file under its lock. Measured on the replay: 50 beats for 304 hook events (16% of events, an 84% cut against a beat per event, 48% fewer than the relay before), with the same final desk through `applyBeat` and every desk at most one window late. Requests per day, against the 100,000 per day Workers free limit (Pages Functions count toward it): before, 2026-10-20 had 23.5k hq-office requests (almost all beats; 4,660 at 19:00 UTC) and 24k Pages Function requests (the same beats forwarded by `/api/office/beat`, plus about 0.5k other calls), about 47.5k (48% of the limit). After, at the replay's ratio, 23.5k x 50 / 97 = about 12.1k beats, so 12.1k hq-office plus 12.1k + 0.5k Pages Function requests, about 24.7k a day (25% of the limit; the 19:00 UTC peak hour about 2.4k hq-office requests). Ceiling: at most 6 beats per minute per busy session plus its final states, 360 per busy session-hour, each 2 requests, so the free limit holds up to 100,000 / 720 = about 138 busy session-hours a day (five sessions busy around the clock is 120). The log has no session ids, so the real cut is an estimate; check the Cloudflare analytics a day after the reinstall. Not changed here: 122 of the logged beats (6%) were `permissionRequest input` (Needs Kurniadi) without a matching `preToolUse` in the 1.5 s check, some 3 ms after a matched one; a follow-up should capture such payloads before changing the permission-check rule.
