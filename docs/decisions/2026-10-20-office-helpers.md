# L1 Agent Office

- Date: 2026-10-20

Owner addition to stage A of the Agent Office revamp ([design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-agent-office-revamp-design.md) section 5.8): sub-agent tracking. The office relay hooks each tool's sub-agent start and stop events (Copilot CLI `subagentStart` / `subagentStop`, Claude Code and Codex `SubagentStart` / `SubagentStop`, each verified before it is installed; a tool without such a hook or without the parent session in its payload is documented and shows nothing) and sends only the number of active helpers per parent session (0 to 9, no names, types, prompts or ids). The beat and the snapshot desk gain an optional `helpers` field in v1 (backward compatible, an owner decision instead of a new contract version; the desk omits it at 0), capped at 9 and decaying after 10 min without a beat. The 3D office draws one small drone per helper orbiting its Rover (at most 4, a "+N" pill on the chip beyond that; instanced in the existing meshes, parked under reduced motion, without glow on lite), and the chip, the drawer, the crew summary and the Classic card read "· 3 helpers" ("· 3 asisten") on the existing line. Helper counts are part of the stage C history log. Kept inside the 40 KB office budget and the main-path budget.
