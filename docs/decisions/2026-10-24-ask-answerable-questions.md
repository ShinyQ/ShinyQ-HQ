Owner request: "think what we can do to upgrade its capability to answer properly especially out of context question but still in a context to answer for example asking about political view since we have data about political view in pdp it can still answer and not rejecting it", and "The political view things was just an example u need to explore more for same out of context things like that still it should be answered instead of rejected". A probe of realistic visitor questions showed keyword refusals of answerable questions (political view, the president's AI plans, AI regulation in the DPR, the health of the tech ecosystem, the age of a project, his manager, learning ML, mixed questions), and model calls wasted on trivia. Decided: personal topics split into HARD (always declined: his vote, parties and candidates, opinions of named people or of an officeholder as a person, religion, family and relationships, his health, pay numbers, personal details) and SOFT topic words that route to an answer. A political-view or civic question is answered from his written policy views with a short partisan note; a mixed question answers its in-scope clauses and declines the private ones in one line; learning questions are answered from his own path, questions about his manager, team or clients from his written teamwork views; off-topic trivia gets a fixed Rover redirect with topic pills and no model call; every decline carries topic pills. Answers tag CONTEXT lines as `[S1]`, a tag counts as a citation only when its sentence uses that line's words, and an answer shows up to two "Read on this site" links. Declined questions now log their PII-scrubbed text (like unanswered ones) plus the route and an over-refusal flag, so the weekly content-gap report shows over-refusals as counts and the owner reads the text locally. Opinions are never invented: gap questions go to the owner and only his written answers become perspectives. The model eval of this change is limited to 20 questions (the boundary set); deterministic routing tests are unlimited. The red team stays at zero unsafe answers. `PROMPT_VERSION` `2026-10-24.answerable.2`.
