# L4 Writing: study guide reader layout

- Date: 2026-10-23

The owner asked for more PDF height in the study guide reader and a more compact header. The two-row toolbar
(Close, title, Chapters, Download on the first row; the page box, Prev, Next and the zoom buttons on the second)
becomes one compact header row on all screens, and the page and zoom controls move into a floating pill at the
bottom center of the pages column (never over the thumbnail rail), in one row down to a 360 px phone. Header and
pill targets stay 44 px on touch screens and shrink to 36 px only with a fine pointer. Measured with the fixture
PDF: the page scroller grows from 709 to 775 px at 1440 x 900 (66 px) and from 727 to 785 px at 390 x 844 (58 px).
