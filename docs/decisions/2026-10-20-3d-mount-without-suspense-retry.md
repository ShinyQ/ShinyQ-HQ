- Date: 2026-10-20

Owner approval: "The owner says yes to feat/perf-mount-early (first frame from about 875 to 640 ms, with the 4 ride baselines updated)" and "rebase onto main, then open the PR with base and head captures of the 4 ride poses and a note on why they changed. The other 44 poses must pass strict and the bytes must not grow." `ExperienceGate` loads the 3D chunk and the 3D page data with `Promise.all` and renders the loaded component directly instead of through `next/dynamic`, whose lazy component suspended once even with its chunk in and held a Suspense retry until 300 ms after its fallback. The first frame (`/en`, returning visitor, `?prefetch=off`, local build, median of 3) went from 674 to 407 ms (full) and 643 to 418 ms (lite). A failed 3D chunk takes the crash recovery path (one automatic retry, then the static page with "Retry 3D", which requests the chunk again) instead of the app error boundary. The 3D bytes are identical to main (first frame full 464.7 KB, lite 448.9 KB gzip; session full 493.6 KB, lite 477.9 KB; the 3D part on the wire is 477,546 B full and 461,241 B lite on both). Classic page JS grows by 1,039 B gzip per gated page (`/en` 188,623 to 189,662 B, `/en/labs` 190,567 to 191,632 B) with no new code: once the gate stops importing `next/dynamic` (DialogHost and AskDock still do), Turbopack groups the same modules into different shared page chunks, which compress worse (raw bytes within about 55 B). Owner decision on that growth: "A: accept the +1.0 KB on Classic (still inside the 240 KB budget, and Classic just saved about 20 KB) in exchange for a first frame about 250 ms sooner. Record it in the decision entry with the chunk-grouping cause." No budget changed.
