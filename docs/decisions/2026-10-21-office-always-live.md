# L1 Agent Office

- Date: 2026-10-21

Owner decision, relayed by the coordinator (Agent Office revamp A2, [design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-agent-office-revamp-design.md) section 5.9, [plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-agent-office-live-ten.md)): the office is always live. This reverses the loading rule of the [revamp decision](2026-10-20-agent-office-revamp.md) (R2: the office chunk only near the office, outside the session totals) and of the [chunks decision](2026-10-20-office-revamp-chunks-contract.md). The office's live layer (the Rovers, the crew animations, the helper drones, the wall screens' content and the beacon) must run whenever the office is in view from anywhere in the Lobby, without driving there. The office chunks load on every L1 visit at idle after the first frame, never on the first-frame path (merging the beacon chunk into the scene chunk is allowed if simpler); they now count in the L1 3D session totals, whose caps rise by the measured office bytes at measured plus 0.6 KB (owner approved about +27 KB). The first-frame cap does not move, and the 40 KB `officeTotal` stays the office chunks' own cap. Runtime: the crew, the drones and the effects pause out of view and step at about 4 fps (a far level of detail) past 40 u from the office; L1 stays under 125 scene draw calls in every view on both tiers, with lite fps healthy. Requests: no per-visitor fast poll; the existing 30 s watch poll while the office is out of view or far, the fast office poll (10 s while a desk is awake, else 30 s) only while the wing is in view and near or a drawer is open; the back-off and the 10 min idle stop are kept. Workers requests per day (free limit 100,000, shared with Ask, stats, visit and RUM; each poll runs the Pages Function once): a busy day of 500 L1 sessions with 4 min far or out of view and 1 min near each gives 500 x 4 min x 2 polls a minute = 4,000 plus 500 x 1 min x 6 polls a minute = 3,000, so about 7,000 visitor polls, plus 2,000 to 6,000 beats and about 1,000 GitHub webhooks (office design section 7): at most about 14,000 a day, 14% of the limit. The far rate equals part A's watch, which already polled every 30 s on every L1 visit, so going always-live adds no far traffic; a forgotten tab stops after 10 min idle (at most 20 polls) and a hidden tab polls nothing; the office alone would reach the limit only at about 6,600 such sessions a day (93,000 left after beats and webhooks / 14 polls a session). The 5 s edge cache per data center (one entry per snapshot version) caps the snapshot reads reaching the Durable Object at one per 5 s per data center and version (at most 17,280 a day each under continuous load, and never more than the polls themselves), on the Durable Object's own 100,000 a day meter; service-binding calls add no request charge. Verification: the ring-south and spawn-turned views show the live office, and an e2e asserts the live layer mounts without moving the rover.
