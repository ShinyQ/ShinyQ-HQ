# Ask the HQ

- Date: 2026-10-31

Ask calibration and the nightly eval. The nightly `ask-eval.yml` (disabled since 2026-10-20) failed for two reasons: a used-up Workers AI or Groq day scored every item as a wrong answer (runs of 2026-10-14, 10-17 and 10-19), and answer checks had false positives (fixed in #273). An item whose every model tier failed on quota, a rate limit, an HTTP error or a timeout now counts as not run: it is left out of the score, and a job fails on coverage only when more than a quarter of its items did not run. The prompt grew from about 2,700 to 3,450 tokens per Groq call, so calls are planned at 3,500 tokens and `openai/gpt-oss-20b` rotates a fourth of the golden set, a twenty-eighth of the pills and a sixth of the red team (worst night 40 calls, 70% of its daily tokens, was 53 calls and over 90%). `allam-2-7b` stops: the prompt fills its 4,096-token context, so it errored or degenerated instead of testing the guards. The answer cache had a 0% hit ratio because its key held the whole-pack version, which changes with every content deploy (about 25 versions in 30 days); it now keys on what the model sees (prompt version, locale, route and the model messages without the pack date line), a served entry passes the output checks again, and hits count against the global cap. The 32% backup share in the week of 2026-10-19 came from evals using up the shared neurons on 10-18 and 10-19 (capped since 2026-10-19); the weekly content-gap report now shows the first model's failure reasons. From the unanswered questions: exes and dating are declined as HARD private topics, a denial such as "has not worked at Google" no longer trips the employer check, and design, data science tools, image generation, hobbies and the code security platform were already answered from existing content; questions about products or employers that are not his stay unanswered.
