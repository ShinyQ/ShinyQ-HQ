# CI: memory gates

- Date: 2026-10-31
- PR: [#362](https://github.com/ShinyQ/ShinyQ-HQ/pull/362)

The `e2e/memory.spec.ts` heap gate measures retained JS, not the heap in use (issue #298). Heap snapshots of the production build found nothing retained per Page View toggle: from toggle 4 to 8 and from 26 to 34 no constructor grew and objects, closures and strings stayed flat, while V8's code grew about 1 MB (the Sparkplug, Maglev and TurboFan tiers as V8 optimizes more of the page; with them off the toggle growth fell from about 270 to 40 KB). The rest was three's per-renderer binding states, keyed by a program id that keeps rising across canvases, which V8 stores in arrays sized to the id until it passes about 1024 (a 417 KB drop at toggle 25). The nightly 10 Lobby to Roof round trips share the cause: `Runtime.getHeapUsage` grew 2.0 to 2.1 MB (1.9 MB of it code) against the 2 MB slack, the retry flake of 2026-10-30. Decided (owner, B2 of the feature audit): the three gates assert `readRetainedJsBytes` (`e2e/heap.ts`: a heap snapshot's objects, closures, arrays, strings and numbers, without V8 code, hidden classes and Blink memory) under the new `GPU_MEMORY_BUDGET.retainedHeapBytes`, 256 KB, in place of the 2 MB on the heap in use, which stays in each annotation and stays the `memory-full` gate (`heapBytes`). Measured locally, 3 runs each: 10 Lobby to Roof trips +32 to 43 KB (heap in use +1,963 to 2,074 KB), 10 B1 trips +16 to 19 KB (+1,067 to 1,339 KB), 4 toggles +93 to 100 KB (+826 to 1,199 KB). The same issue's stale program is fixed at its cause: the boot precompile (`ProgramWarmup`, frame 3) compiled L2's pads before their passive effect set instance colors, and now leaves a floor's content to its `FloorPrep` until that began (the rest of the scene still compiles in one call: a subtree compiled on its own adds its lights again, and the rover's headlight then gave the full tier about 7 extra programs a floor); over 20 toggles with V8's optimizing tiers off (where the race hit about 60% of mounts) every mount held 41 programs, so the toggle gate no longer retries mounts to match `pinOnlyPrograms`, and the full-tier floor program counts match a main build.
