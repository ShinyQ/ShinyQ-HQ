`/journey` opens with Time Travel: the owner's journey as a skyline of 9 stops (Before 2019, then 2019 to 2026), each building made of one block per item (roles, study and milestones, projects, awards, writing and research, certifications), opened on now with a Play button that sweeps from the prologue to now. Owner choices: what it scrubs (the journey, not the site's build history), Classic only (no 3D bytes), the skyline centerpiece, accumulating stops, curated highlights and one caption per stop in the owner's voice. The owner approved all 9 captions as written, in English and Indonesian, on 2026-10-25; they are now final content. Delegated to the parent session (D12 to D15 in the design): highlights are career entries, projects (pods), papers and certifications (awards through their year's trophy case entry), the URL never changes while scrubbing, the captions stay out of the Ask pack, and the caption drafts were proposals until the owner approved them.
