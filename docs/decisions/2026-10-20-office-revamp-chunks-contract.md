# L1 Agent Office

- Date: 2026-10-20

Owner decisions during stage A of the Agent Office revamp ([design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-agent-office-revamp-design.md)), in line with the budget principle that the session caps grow only by a small loader: (1) the main 3D path keeps only a small loader; the office watch (the 30 s poll and the signal) and the minimap cue live in an office watch chunk loaded after the first frame goes idle on L1 (it loads on every L1 session, so it counts in the 3D session totals, with its own cap at the measured value plus 0.6 KB), and the Needs Kurniadi beacon mesh lives in a beacon chunk loaded only the first time the watch sees an asking desk and kept for the session (its own cap, outside the session total, like the office chunk); stage B's deploy beam goes into the beacon chunk. (2) The minimap cue covers the views that face away from the wing (the hall road, the elevator arrival, portrait screens at the spawn); no HUD edge arrow. (3) Old clients never see a new field: the sub-agent `helpers` count is served only in snapshot v2 (`GET /api/office?v=2`, the v1 snapshot plus an optional desk `helpers`), which the 3D scene and the Classic card built after the change request; `GET /api/office` (v1) stays byte for byte, with a test that a v1 response never carries `helpers`. Stage B's pipeline becomes v3 (v2 plus `pipeline`) for the 3D scene only.
