# L4 Writing: reader tracks

- Date: 2026-10-25

Owner-approved revamp of the L4 Writing floor around reader tracks (design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-l4-writing-revamp-design.md`). D1: the primary visitor outcome is finding the post worth reading fast (relevance over recency). D2: the visitor picks an interest: reader tracks near the arrival, each led by its best post. D3: a new small set of reader tracks (4 today, 1 to 6 allowed in content), each post in exactly one track, separate from the 13 constellation topics. D4: the lead post is an optional owner pick per track, falling back to the newest post of the track. D5: up to about 60 posts in-world with no archive fallback (layout tested to 120). D6: the tracks replace the timeline of single-post stations, the archive board and the year posts; the press plaza (headline wall, featured stage), the desks and the end plaza stay. D7: bytes target neutral by deleting what the tracks replace; a measured gap may ask the owner for at most +1.5 KB gzip. D8: 3D only in this PR; the track data (`buildTrackIndex`) is built so the Classic `/library` page can adopt it later. D9: approach A, track shelves: one bookshelf bay per track with instanced book spines and the lead face-out, on a book kit shared with the Study Vault (no new shader program); a spine click drives to the bay door and opens the post, a bay door opens the track drawer, one hint bubble per bay. Bytes (owner order 2026-10-25): the shelf slots and the spine targets moved off the main 3D path into the lazy L4 chunk (`slots.ts`, the targets registered by `Library`), and the shelves dropped the light pools under the leads and the topic brightening on spines (a picked topic still lights its bays through the beacons). Measured on the branch: the main 3D path full 477,260 B and lite 461,126 B (12 B under main; 334 B under `failAt` 477,594 B); the L4 chunk 11,894 B to 13,672 B gzip (cap 16 KB); the 3D session total lite 527,974 B and full 544,108 B, over the caps (526,266 B and 542,400 B) by 1,708 B on both tiers and over the +1.5 KB ceiling (527,802 B and 543,936 B) by 172 B, pending an owner budget note.
