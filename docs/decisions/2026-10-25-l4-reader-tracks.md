# L4 Writing: reader tracks

- Date: 2026-10-25

Owner-approved revamp of the L4 Writing floor around reader tracks (design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-l4-writing-revamp-design.md`). D1: the primary visitor outcome is finding the post worth reading fast (relevance over recency). D2: the visitor picks an interest: reader tracks near the arrival, each led by its best post. D3: a new small set of reader tracks (4 today, 1 to 6 allowed in content), each post in exactly one track, separate from the 13 constellation topics. D4: the lead post is an optional owner pick per track, falling back to the newest post of the track. D5: up to about 60 posts in-world with no archive fallback (layout tested to 120). D6: the tracks replace the timeline of single-post stations, the archive board and the year posts; the press plaza (headline wall, featured stage), the desks and the end plaza stay. D7: bytes target neutral by deleting what the tracks replace; a measured gap may ask the owner for at most +1.5 KB gzip. D8: 3D only in this PR; the track data (`buildTrackIndex`) is built so the Classic `/library` page can adopt it later. D9: approach A, track shelves: one bookshelf bay per track with instanced book spines and the lead face-out, on a book kit shared with the Study Vault (no new shader program); a spine click drives to the bay door and opens the post, a bay door opens the track drawer, one hint bubble per bay. Measured on the branch: the L4 chunk 11,894 B to 13,251 B gzip (cap 16 KB); the main 3D path +311 B gzip (full 477,583 B under `failAt` 477,594 B); the 3D session total over its caps by 1,610 B on both tiers, left for a separate owner budget note.
