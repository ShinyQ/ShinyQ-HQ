# L4 Writing: reader tracks

- Date: 2026-10-25

Owner-approved revamp of the L4 Writing floor around reader tracks (design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-l4-writing-revamp-design.md`). D1: the primary visitor outcome is finding the post worth reading fast (relevance over recency). D2: the visitor picks an interest: reader tracks near the arrival, each led by its best post. D3: a new small set of reader tracks (4 today, 1 to 6 allowed in content), each post in exactly one track, separate from the 13 constellation topics. D4: the lead post is an optional owner pick per track, falling back to the newest post of the track. D5: up to about 60 posts in-world with no archive fallback (layout tested to 120). D6: the tracks replace the timeline of single-post stations, the archive board and the year posts; the press plaza (headline wall, featured stage), the desks and the end plaza stay. D7: bytes target neutral by deleting what the tracks replace; a measured gap may ask the owner for at most +1.5 KB gzip. D8: 3D only in this PR; the track data (`buildTrackIndex`) is built so the Classic `/library` page can adopt it later. D9: approach A, track shelves: one bookshelf bay per track with instanced book spines and the lead face-out, on a book kit shared with the Study Vault (no new shader program); a spine click drives to the bay door and opens the post, a bay door opens the track drawer, one hint bubble per bay. Bytes (owner order 2026-10-25): the shelf slots and the spine targets moved off the main 3D path into the lazy L4 chunk (`slots.ts`, the targets registered by `Library`), and the shelves dropped the light pools under the leads and the topic brightening on spines (a picked topic still lights its bays through the beacons). Session budget (owner approved, 2026-10-25): trim first without feature loss, then raise the session caps; the owner first allowed at most +1.5 KB per tier, then approved the raise past that ceiling, set by the budgets rule (measured + 614 B). Trims: the leads' light pools and the topic brightening on spines dropped (above); the shelf slots and spine targets moved into the L4 chunk; one sign list pass with each bay's slots built once and post titles by slug (`postTitles`, without the unread desk titles); a leaner `baySlots`; the label frame's picked and live checks without kind tests; one shared `setCursor`; the turning-book GLSL written without spaces. Measured: the main 3D path full 477,249 B and lite 461,115 B (`failAt` 477,594 B unchanged, 345 B under on full); the L4 chunk 11,894 B on main to 13,519 B gzip (cap 16 KB unchanged); the 3D session total lite 527,810 B and full 543,944 B. Session caps: lite 526,266 B to 528,424 B and full 542,400 B to 544,558 B, +2,158 B on both tiers; `failAt` and every other cap stay.
