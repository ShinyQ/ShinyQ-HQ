# 3D experience

- Date: 2026-10-29
- PR: [#348](https://github.com/ShinyQ/ShinyQ-HQ/pull/348)

Phone-first 3D, part 3 (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-phone-first-3d-part-3-design.md`, owner decisions 2026-10-28/29): landscape phones (wider than tall, under 500 px high) get their own camera class `landscape`, held to the same reading rule as portrait phones (every L2 to RF title and name at least 10 CSS px at 844 x 390 and 9 px at 667 x 375). On phones the joystick floats: a drag that starts in the lower-left drive zone (portrait: the left half of the bottom 40%; landscape: the left 40% below the floor chip row) spawns the stick under the thumb and drives, a still tap there still selects a glowing target, and the rest of the screen looks; tablets keep the fixed stick. Boost moves bottom-right above Reset view on phones. No new gestures. A one-time tilt hint (EN "Tip: turn your screen sideways for a wider view of the tower." / ID "Tips: putar layar ke posisi mendatar supaya tampilan menara lebih luas.") follows the tour's closing line on portrait touch phones and tablets, clears on a tap or a rotation and never locks the orientation; its strings ride in the tour chunk. Budgets: the landscape camera and the floating stick cost about 1.3 KB on the main path, so the touch controls (the tablet joystick, the floating stick and its gesture, Boost) load as their own chunk on coarse pointers only, outside both session totals, with a new cap `BYTES_3D_BUDGET.touch` (measured 2,368 B, cap 2,982 B); the tour chunk cap is re-set for the tilt hint (measured 1,340 B, cap 1,954 B). Measured against a main build on the same machine: first frame lite 459,737 B and full 475,871 B (main 459,957 B and 476,091 B, 220 B less), session totals lite 526,692 B and full 542,826 B (main 526,882 B and 543,016 B, 190 B less); floor chunks L3 14,416 B, L4 13,648 B, RF 3,431 B (12, 13 and 5 B more, under their caps). Both caps approved by the owner (relayed by the parent session, 2026-10-29). No other cap changed.
