# L1 Agent Office

- Date: 2026-10-20

Owner decisions on the Agent Office wing's view ([wing decision](2026-10-20-agent-office-wing.md)). (1) "Add the look offset": the shared follow rig takes an optional per-zone look-target offset (`ViewZone.lookOffset` or a class view's own, `{ forward?, up? }` in u, scaled with the follow distance; `followPose` moves only the look target, the camera stays), eased in and out with the zone like its zoom (a hand zoom or a reset eases it out, reduced motion never applies it). It is opt-in data used only by the `L1:office` zone; every other zone produces float-for-float the poses of before (`tests/unit/camera-look-offset.test.ts` against a frozen copy of the old rig and orbit, the pinned intro poses untouched). The portrait view (the mobile camera class: 390 x 844 and 820 x 1180) moves its look target 6.25 u toward the camera at pitch 0.2 (was 0.1, zoom 1.3 kept): the sky over the wall frame drops from 38.2% to 20.4% with the five desks and the Rover in frame; desktop and tablet views are unchanged (33.1%, 32.7%). The view tilting down reaches closer to the camera, so the wing sign moves from S - 2 to S - 5 over the lead road to stay under the bottom edge. (2) Option (a): the ghost outline of the floors above (the amber L2 line across the office view) hides only while the rover is in the office wing (`FloorLayout.hideGhostAbove` on L1) or a view zone flagged `hideGhostAbove` (the office stop); it stays everywhere else, the spawn included. It fades over the ghost floors' own fade (the first person glide, 0.45 s) and cuts under reduced motion; the ghost fill is unchanged, and no draw call or program is added (a material opacity). Both changes ride the main path (the rig, the orbit, the tower), the office chunks unchanged (scene 14,290 B, drawer body 5,996 B); by the owner budget rule (measured plus about 0.6 KB) the 3D caps are re-set: first frame 462.2 KB (measured 461.6 KB full, 445.9 KB lite; was 461.6), session full 491.1 KB (measured 490.5 KB; was 490.6) and lite 475.4 KB (measured 474.8 KB; was 474.8). L1 settled scene calls are unchanged: 101 lite and 103 full at the spawn, 51 and 53 at the office stop.
