# Ask the HQ

- Date: 2026-10-24

Second part of the answerable-questions decision: Ask retrieval adds multilingual semantic search next to BM25. Every knowledge chunk and the router exemplars get a Workers AI `@cf/baai/bge-m3` embedding (1024 dimensions, English and Indonesian in one space), int8-quantized and committed as a cache keyed by a hash of the model and text (`content/generated/ask-embeddings.json`, filled by `bun scripts/ask-embed.ts`; the deploy fills any missing vector with `CF_EVAL_TOKEN`); the stored KV value carries them (730.6 KB of the 768 KiB cap at decision time). A question costs one embedding call (about 0.03 neurons, 300 ms at most); retrieval fuses the BM25 and cosine rankings by reciprocal rank, with question templates folded out of BM25 and EN/ID topic synonyms added; an error, a timeout or a pack without vectors keeps BM25 alone, logged as `retrieval` `bm25` against `hybrid`. The same vector re-routes a question the rules left on the default route to the learning, civic or off-topic route only when one label clearly leads, and a strong private similarity blocks any re-route: similarity never declines and never answers a private topic. Embedding the whole pack cost about 30 neurons. Conversation memory grows from 3 to 5 signed turns (body cap 12 KiB, about 48 neurons for a five-turn follow-up) and understands spoken Indonesian follow-ups ("terus", "gimana kalau", "yang tadi").
