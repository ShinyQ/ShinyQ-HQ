- Date: 2026-10-28

Phone-first 3D, part 2 (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-phone-first-3d-part-2-design.md`, owner decisions 2026-10-28): floor framing and legibility plus the keyboard hints are part 2; joystick on demand, gestures and a landscape camera go to part 3. On a phone a Rover line without choices is a top toast (portrait 144 px down, below the minimap row; landscape 16 px down, between the floor chip and the Ask and menu buttons) clamped at four rows, so every floor line shows whole at 375 px in EN and ID (the spec's three rows grew to four for the longest Indonesian line). A phone reading rule: every title and name on L2, L3, L4 and RF is at least 10 CSS px tall at 390 x 844 and 9 px at 375 x 667 from its reading stop, fixed with phone zone views first and portrait-only type sizes second; fine print is exempt. Touch screens see no keyboard keys: labels drop their keys, key caps hide on coarse pointers with `aria-keyshortcuts` on the control, key-only hint lines get touch variants. Budgets: the Corners menu sheet body and (owner approval relayed by the parent session the same day, because L2's portrait type on the main path and the floor chunks' phone sizes would otherwise raise the totals) the pad picker sheet move into lazy chunks loaded on first use, outside both session totals like the tour chunk, with new caps `BYTES_3D_BUDGET.menu` (measured 1,470 B, cap 2,084 B) and `BYTES_3D_BUDGET.picker` (measured 1,891 B, cap 2,505 B). Measured against a main build on the same machine: first frame full 476,048 B and lite 459,914 B (main 476,875 B and 460,741 B, 827 B less), session totals full 542,978 B and lite 526,844 B (main 543,597 B and 527,463 B, 619 B less); floor chunks L3 14,409 B, L4 13,635 B, RF 3,426 B (44, 89 and 75 B more, under their caps).
